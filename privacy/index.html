Privacy policy

Last updated: 26 October 2026 · Deutsche Fassung (maßgeblich)

This privacy policy applies to all apps and games by Mythpip Games. Part A applies to all apps, Part B describes for each app which data it processes. This is a translation; the German version is legally binding. Provider and controller: see Legal notice.

Part A – All apps

A1. Controller

The controller responsible for data processing is the provider named in the Legal Notice (email: mythpipgames@gmail.com).

A2. Principle

Our apps require no registration and no user account unless stated otherwise in Part B. Save games, settings and progress are stored only locally on your device; we do not receive them and do not operate our own servers. Uninstalling an app deletes its local data. We only receive personal data ourselves if you write to us (A6).

A3. Downloading via Google Play

When you download an app, Google (Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland) processes data such as your Google account and device information as an independent controller. We have no influence on this. Google's privacy policy applies: https://policies.google.com/privacy

A4. In-app purchases via Google Play

If an app offers purchases (see Part B), payment is handled exclusively by Google Play. We receive no payment data, only a confirmation of which purchase belongs to your Google account so the app can unlock and restore it. Legal basis: performance of the purchase contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).

A5. Advertising with Google AdMob

If an app shows ads (see Part B), we use Google AdMob, a service of Google (address see A3). Google processes the data for advertising as an independent controller.

Data processed: your device's advertising ID (Android advertising ID), IP address, device information (e.g. model, operating system, language), approximate location based on the IP address, and information about which ads were shown and tapped.

Purposes: delivering and billing ads, measuring ad performance, preventing fraud and – only with your consent – showing personalised ads.

Legal bases: We ask for your consent to store and read information on your device (e.g. the advertising ID) (Section 25(1) TDDDG); the processing based on it, in particular personalised advertising, is also based on your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). If you do not consent, Google only shows so-called limited ads: no information is stored on or read from your device for them and there is no personalised advertising. The processing required for this (e.g. of the IP address for delivery and fraud prevention) is based on our legitimate interest in financing the free app (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

Retention: Google decides how long it stores this data; details are in Google's privacy policy.

Transfers to third countries: Google may also transfer data to the USA. Google is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework, for which the EU Commission has found an adequate level of data protection.

Consent and withdrawal: On first launch the app asks for your consent – where required by law, in particular in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland – via a Google window (User Messaging Platform). You can change or withdraw your choice at any time in the app (see Part B for where); the app then reloads its ads with your new choice. Withdrawal applies to the future. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in the Android settings.

Ad content: We only allow ads that Google rates as suitable for teenagers.

More information:

  • Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • How Google uses data from partner apps: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

A6. Contact by email (feedback)

If you email us – including via the "Send Feedback" button in an app – we process your email address, your name (if given) and the content of your message. The button also pre-fills the app version, device model and selected language into the text so we can reproduce bugs; you see this information before sending and can delete it. The app itself sends nothing – your mail app only sends once you tap "Send".

Purpose and legal basis: answering your request and improving the app (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR); if your request concerns a purchase, performance of the contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).

Recipient: Our mailbox is hosted by Google (Gmail, address see A3).

Retention: We delete your message once your request has been dealt with, at the latest after 12 months – unless statutory retention obligations (e.g. for business letters) require longer storage.

A7. Discord

Some apps link to our Discord server. Only when you tap the button does Discord or your browser open; the app itself transmits nothing to Discord. Discord itself is responsible for everything that happens on Discord. Discord's privacy policy applies: https://discord.com/privacy – Discord has its own minimum age (at least 13, depending on the country).

A8. Children and teenagers

Our apps are not directed at children unless stated otherwise in Part B. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. In Germany, only persons aged 16 or over can give consent to advertising (A5) themselves (Art. 8 GDPR; other countries partly have other age limits); if you are younger, your parents or guardians decide. Without consent the app only shows limited ads (A5).

A9. Your rights

You have the right of access (Art. 15 GDPR), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction of processing (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20) and to object to processing based on legitimate interests (Art. 21). You can withdraw consent at any time (Art. 7(3)).

We only hold personal data about you if you have written to us (A6). Requests about data processed by Google or Discord are best addressed directly to these providers; you can, however, always contact us as well (contact in the Legal Notice).

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority, in particular in the country where you live.

A10. This website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA. When you visit it, GitHub processes technically necessary data – in particular your IP address, date and time of access and information about your browser and operating system – to deliver the site and to fend off attacks; for this GitHub keeps server logs. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in a secure, working website (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). GitHub may transfer data to the USA and is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework. GitHub's privacy statement applies: https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement

This website sets no cookies, uses no analytics or tracking tools and loads no content from other servers – fonts, images and videos are hosted on the site itself. Links to Discord, TikTok, YouTube, Instagram and Reddit are plain links: only when you click one do you leave this site, and the privacy policy of that provider applies.

A11. Changes

We update this privacy policy when new apps are added, an app changes or the law changes. The version published here applies.
