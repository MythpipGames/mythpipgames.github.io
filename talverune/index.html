Choose your hero

Start with three heroes and unlock two more as you progress. Every class has its own abilities, animations and gear.

Mage

Ranged spells – Fireball, Chain Lightning, Frost Nova.

Warrior

Melee with high HP – Whirlwind, Shield Bash, Charge.

Archer

Fast and ranged – Quick Shot, Piercing Arrow, Poison Arrow.

Necromancer

Curses and an army of the dead – Raise Dead, Bone Spear, Soul Drain.
Unlock as you play

Assassin

Swift melee – Shadow Strike, Poison Blade, Smoke Bomb.
Unlock as you play

What awaits you

Endless waves

Every wave is a little tougher than the last. Fight through a stone dungeon, a poison forest, a fire cave and an ice temple – your run only ends when you fall.

Boss fights

Face the Stone Guardian, the Inferno Knight and the Void Archmage – each with their own attacks to read and dodge.

Nightmare mode

Reach wave 50 to unlock the Nightmare: elite enemies, five Nightmare bosses – and later, two bosses at once.

Loot and Legacies

Gear in five rarity tiers, class sets and permanent Legacies that make every new run go a little deeper.

Made for phones

Touch controls with a floating joystick, played in landscape. No account needed – and if a run is interrupted, just continue later.

6 languages

English, German, Spanish, Portuguese, French and Russian.
